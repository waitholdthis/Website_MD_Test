# Church website review

Use this checklist for requests from the Tootie Designs church website. The email reply should include three changes that make a first visit easier. Aim to complete the review and draft the reply in 20 minutes.

## Intake

- Record the church name, public website URL, reply address, and date received.
- Open the site on a phone. Start at the homepage and use the site as a visitor would. Check the intended campus if the church has more than one.
- If the site is unavailable, confirm the address with the requester before reviewing. If the church has no website, ask where it currently publishes service information and tailor the recommendations to that starting point.

## Review the visitor journey

| Check | What to try | What to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service times | Find the next regular service from the homepage. Check whether times are easy to read and consistent across the site. | Where the times appear, how many taps it takes, and any conflicting information. |
| Directions | Find the address and open the directions link. For multiple campuses, check that service times, addresses, and links are paired correctly. | The page or link that caused hesitation. Note whether parking or entrance guidance would help. |
| What to expect | Find a visitor page or clear guidance about arriving, accessibility, and children's check-in, where applicable. | One question a newcomer would still have based on the information published. |
| Mobile usability | Repeat those tasks using the mobile menu. Check that text, buttons, and links are usable and that overlays do not block the next action. | The specific page and action affected. |

## Pick three actions

1. Put a broken or conflicting detail first when it could send someone to the wrong place or at the wrong time.
2. For each recommendation, name the page, what you observed, and the specific change. Mark anything the church should confirm, such as its current service schedule.
3. Prioritize changes a church staff member or volunteer can act on. If you find fewer than three clear problems, use an opportunity to make the visit easier and label it as an improvement.

## Example findings

These illustrate the level of detail to send. They are fictional.

1. **Put the Sunday service time on the homepage.** I found the address at the top of the page, but I had to open the calendar to find the regular service. Put the verified time beside the address, especially on mobile.
2. **Link directions from the visitor page.** The address appears under Contact. Add a Directions button beside the service time and link to the correct map pin.
3. **Explain children's check-in.** The visitor page says children's ministry is available. Add where parents go, when check-in opens, and what happens when they arrive. Confirm those details with the ministry lead first.
