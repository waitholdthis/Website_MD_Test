01 — The idea

A house style is a shortcut. We think your brand deserves the long way — a site that could only ever belong to you.

Most studios have a signature you can spot from across the room. That’s efficient for them and quietly limiting for you: the palette, the grid, the same tidy sans-serif on every project. It photographs well in a portfolio and blends into everyone else’s.

We work the other way around. Every engagement starts from your audience, your voice and the job the site actually has to do — then the typography, colour and motion are drawn to fit. The through-line across our work isn’t a look. It’s the care.

03 — The process

Four steps, start to launch.

The order never changes, even though the outcome always does. Here is exactly how a Tootie project moves from a first conversation to a site that’s live.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We dig into your audience, your competitors and the one thing the site truly has to accomplish. No design happens yet — just sharp questions and a clear brief.

  2. 02

    Design

    Typography, colour, layout and motion are drawn from scratch for you. You see real screens with real content, not a template with your logo dropped in.

  3. 03

    Build

    Hand-built, accessible front-ends — fast, responsive and typed all the way down. Every interaction is deliberate and every edge case is handled.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We ship with SSL, hardened headers, analytics and a walkthrough so you can run it yourself. Then we stay close for the first stretch of real traffic.

05 — The studio

Small on purpose. You work with the person doing the work.

“A brief is a person on the other end, not a checklist. My job is to build the site they’d have made themselves — if they did this for a living.”

Tootie Designs is an independent studio led by Parker Tootill. Because it’s intentionally small, there are no account managers, no hand-offs and no house style to defend — just direct collaboration from the first sketch to launch day.

It also means saying no when a project isn’t the right fit, so the ones we take on get the attention they deserve. If that sounds like the way you’d want your site made, we should talk.
